Mammoth, CA

  • With Shiv, Noah, Stephanie
  • Jan 2025
  • Mammoth Lakes, California

Noah's annual birthday trip in 2025 was in Mammoth, at his parents' place. It had insane proximity to one of the main lifts. You could literally walk there within five minutes. There was no fresh snow, but the vibes were A+. We got to check out a few different resorts, a few different restaurants, and a lot of après.

Day One: Getting to Mammoth

Picture #2

I got in and drove up to Mammoth right after the new year. The forecast had said it was going to start snowing. I had brought chains just in case, but I had not really thought about the possibility of it actually snowing.

As I got to town it started snowing a little. I decided I needed to get my skis done, so I dropped them off at a nearby ski shop. By the time I got out, the snowfall had picked up a lot. Driving the Honda CRV without snow tires was becoming precarious. The car was sliding, and the wheels were not gaining traction.

I started driving toward Noah's place, which was up on a hill. Thankfully I pulled over right before a one lane highway, because the car was not doing a good job of getting traction, and put the chains on. I am so thankful I did. It was so slick. The car would not have made it up that hill, and I would have been stuck on a one way highway trying to put on chains I had never used before, with nobody able to get around me.

I spent about 30 minutes figuring the chains out and watched a YouTube video on low service. Eventually I found my way to Noah's place, where Shivam was already there. I had not seen Shivam in a very long time. We always seem to catch each other on Noah's ski trips, and this was no exception.

We ate sushi that first night, and some ramen. Then I took a nice hot bath in one of the bathtubs at Noah's place. What a great way to relax after a good meal.

Shivam and I got out to the mountain first. Noah and Stephanie were arriving later that day, so we got up, hit the slopes, and got a few runs in, all the way to the backside. Then I had to make my way to the front side on my own so I could go pick them up.

They landed at Bishop Airport. It was only about an hour drive, and I needed something to do on the way, so I started listening to crime podcasts, which I had never really done before. They were pretty interesting in the moment. I never picked it up as a permanent thing, but it was a great way to pass the time.

Once I got to the airport, Noah and Stephanie were already waiting. I was probably 20 minutes after they had landed. We made our way back to Mammoth, grabbed a quick lunch and a beer, and got a few more runs in before the day ended.

Shivam took the next day off, so Noah, Stephanie, and I went to a resort a little farther away called June. It reminded me a lot of Deer Valley. Smaller, with wide open groomed runs. Not as many tree runs, though there were some, and practically no lines. The snow still was not fantastic, but the vibes were unreal. We took a long lunch break, hung around, and got photos on the deck.

As the day wrapped up we went to one of Noah's favorite restaurants in Mammoth, Dos Alas. It is a Puerto Rican restaurant, and the meal was incredible. Every dish came with meat and rice, and the meats were cooked perfectly.

Shivam was back the next day, and we hit Mammoth Mountain again. It was beautiful and sunny, and we spent the day on the back and the front of the mountain. The snow was pretty hard packed. I am not as good as Shivam and Noah, so I was not quite staying with them, but we still got some good skiing in.

After that we drove to a nearby bar Noah was very excited about. I had a Bee's Knees with a yellow flower, made with one of the best gins I have had. It was infused with lemon. It was fantastic, and the place was extremely cozy.
