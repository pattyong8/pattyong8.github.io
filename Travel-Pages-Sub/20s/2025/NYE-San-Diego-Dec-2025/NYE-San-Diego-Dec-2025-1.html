NYE San Diego

  • With Cal Friends
  • Dec 31st 2025
  • San Diego, California

For the past couple of years, we had a tradition of renting an Airbnb in San Diego with the New York crew for New Year's Eve. That did not happen this year, but instead, I got to celebrate with some of my Cal friends from college. Jack could not make it, but we got to catch up with Rishi, who had recently moved to San Diego, and spend New Year's Eve with the rest of the fellas. A great way to ring in the new year!

Matt and I had gone back and forth on whether to stay in Southern California for New Year's Eve or head back to our respective cities. In the end, we decided to stick around and carpooled down to San Diego together. We kicked off the trip at a brewery, enjoyed a few beers, and then met up with Rishi for dinner.

Rishi had recently moved down from San Francisco, and he took us to a restaurant he had been wanting to bring his family to. It was great to catch up over some good food.

After dinner, we stopped by Rishi's place for another drink before heading to Connor's. Funny enough, Connor was not even there when we arrived. Instead, we were greeted by Ryan, Quinn, Jared, Harry, Sam, Emily, and a few other friends. We spent the night playing drinking games, catching up, and having a great time ringing in the new year.

The next morning, Matt, Connor, Rishi, and I decided to hit the gym for a little recovery session. Rishi was hilarious, jumping around like nothing had happened the night before and doing some of the highest box jumps I had ever seen. A fun way to recover after a long night and wrap up another great New Year's trip.
