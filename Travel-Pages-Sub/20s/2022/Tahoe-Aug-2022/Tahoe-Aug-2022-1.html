Tahoe with ES Besties

  • With ES Besties & San Francisco friends
  • August 12th to 14th 2022
  • Lake Tahoe, California

This was a wonderful summer trip with my ES besties and some of our other San Francisco friends. We hiked, we drank, we ate, and we laughed. It was a wonderful way to get outdoors and spend some time with the people I care about. This was also my first really memorable summer trip to Lake Tahoe, and it definitely set the bar high.

We started the trip appropriately prepared with a pretty ridiculous amount of wine, our way of making sure we were ready to have a good time.

One of the highlights of the trip was a pretty steep hike up one of the nearby mountains. As we got closer to the top, we came across some rocks that absolutely did not require any scrambling. But, being a rock climber, I obviously wanted to climb them anyway. I somehow convinced the entire group to come along with me, which turned into a really fun little detour and gave me an excuse to embrace my climbing side.

Eventually, we made it to the viewpoint overlooking Lake Tahoe, and it was absolutely beautiful. I had never seen Tahoe from that vantage point before, and it made the climb completely worth it. Naturally, we turned the top into a full photo shoot before eventually making our way back down.

Later that day, we headed to the lake to cool off after the hike. Joan had told us about a cliff-jumping spot nearby, so we swam over to check it out. We didn't bring our phones with us, so unfortunately there aren't any photos of the actual jumps, but I decided to jump from the very, very top.

Turns out water gets pretty hard when you hit it from high enough. I landed with my hand completely outstretched and came away with a gnarly red mark and bruise from smacking the water. It eventually disappeared, and thankfully that was the extent of the damage, but it was a pretty good reminder that there is, in fact, a wrong way to land in water.

Back at the house, we cooked up an absolute storm. Esther made amazing Korean galbi, and we had kimchi and all the fixings, including scallions, lettuce, cucumber salad, and plenty of other food. After a full day of hiking, climbing, swimming, and cliff jumping, it was exactly the meal we needed.

One of my favorite random photos from the trip is just the ES besties standing around flossing our teeth together afterward. Nothing particularly exciting, but somehow a perfect little snapshot of the trip.

The next day was all about taking it easy and enjoying Tahoe. We spent the day on the sandy shore, soaking up the sun and taking in how beautiful it was outside.

At one point, we turned jumping off the dock into another full photo shoot. It wasn't high or remotely scary, but we had way too much fun trying to coordinate jumps, spell things out in the air, and make increasingly ridiculous poses together.

We also took a bunch of film photos throughout the day, which captured some of my favorite little moments from the trip. This was one of my first times really spending a lot of time with Artem and Olivia, which made the weekend even more memorable.

We played games, ate really good food, spent plenty of time by the water, and just enjoyed being together. Nothing overly complicated. Just a good old-fashioned Tahoe summer trip with a really great group of people.
