Vincent & Erin's Wedding

  • With Family & Friends
  • Nov 6th-11th, 2024
  • Palm Springs, CA

What a wonderful and deeply special weekend celebrating two of the most important people in my life, Vincent and Erin. From the very first moments in Palm Springs, the energy felt different in the best way possible. There was excitement in the air, but also this calm sense that everyone was exactly where they were meant to be. Family flew in from around the world, friends came in from every chapter of life, and every shared meal, conversation, and laugh felt like a reminder of how much love surrounded this wedding. The setting was breathtaking, the weather seemed to cooperate at every turn, and each day built beautifully toward the next. It was one of those rare weekends where time moved fast, memories stacked up quickly, and you could feel in real time that you were living through something unforgettable. Truly a memory for the books.

Arrival & Pre-trip (Nov 2–6)

Our grandfather and cousin flew in from Taiwan a few weeks before the wedding, which made this entire trip feel even more meaningful from the start. Before heading into wedding mode, we spent time together in Newport Beach celebrating my mom's birthday and enjoying everyday moments that ended up becoming some of our favorite memories. We took a Duffy ride around Balboa, had dinner at Taiko, one of my mom's favorite sushi restaurants, and finished with Sprinkles cupcakes from Vincent and Erin. It was the perfect way to begin. Vincent also had the brilliant idea to get to Palm Springs early, and it made all the difference. Instead of feeling rushed, we got to settle in, explore the city and surrounding areas, and really enjoy being together as a family. One of the funniest details from this stretch was the steak dinner Vincent and I made. Charles loved it so much that when he went back to Taiwan, he told everyone about it and gave all the credit to my mom. Later, when relatives visited her, they asked for that same steak, which still makes us laugh. We also spent an unexpectedly long time at the Oasis area balancing rocks on a mini hill for almost an hour, trying to build the perfect stack. Another core memory was the casino stop: even though our grandfather was not a fan of gambling, Vincent and I each put in twenty dollars, played exactly one hand, and won while everyone watched. We were so pumped that we used the winnings to buy In-N-Out for everyone that night.

Pre-Wedding Activities

The days leading into the wedding were packed with laughter, movement, and that perfect mix of calm and anticipation. We explored Joshua Tree and took the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway up to the top, where my brother and I learned the hard way that shirts and shorts were not enough once the temperature dropped. We spent half the time ducking inside to stay warm, which everyone found hilarious. Back at the house, the rhythm of the trip really came together with home-cooked meals, ping pong battles, video games, and quality family time. Once we settled into Bougainvillea, the weekend became real. The crew arrived, tables and lights started going up, and you could feel the scale of the celebration coming into focus. Rehearsal with both parties was smooth and emotional, and the rehearsal dinner that night was one long highlight reel of speeches, reconnections, and stories from every era of Vincent and Erin's lives. One moment that stood out was seeing Kathy give Vincent such a generous gift. Knowing she had come all the way from Taiwan and still wanted to make that moment so special added another layer of meaning to an already emotional night.

The all-day party energy around the weekend was a blast and gave everyone time to just enjoy each other before the main event. Parth and Quinn basically lived on the pickleball court and loved every minute of it, which made total sense because they are huge racket-sport fans. I did not go in the pool, but I spent the day circulating, catching up with people, meeting new folks, eating plenty of tacos, and enjoying a lot of good drinks and good vibes. I got in a few pickleball games too, with Brendan Houston's mom, with Parth, and with Kathy, my mom, and Doug for a few rounds. We also played a lot of beer die, and Parth and I ended up winning a bunch of games together even though I had not played in a while. Those hours were relaxed, funny, and full of great side conversations, and they set the tone for an amazing celebration ahead.

Wedding Day

Wedding day arrived with a mix of excitement, nerves, and gratitude that is hard to put into words. Vincent stayed with me the night before, and by morning everything shifted into ceremony mode. The groomsmen gathered, photographers started capturing every detail, and the game room turned into our getting-ready headquarters. I helped Vincent with his tux, took photos with family, and had one of those moments where you realize how important the day really is. I was also trying to rehearse my best man speech throughout the day because I was honestly very nervous and wanted to do a great job for both of them. Before we headed out, Erin sent me to grab Vincent a shot to settle the nerves, and that quick pre-ceremony moment became one of my favorite memories because it was so human and real. At the venue, everything looked immaculate, and the weather could not have been better. Standing up front as best man, holding the rings, and walking with my brother into one of the most important moments of his life was emotional in the best way. It was a once-in-a-lifetime feeling I will never forget.

The Ceremony

When Vincent got to the front and the ceremony truly started, all I could do was look around and take in how many important people in our lives were gathered in one place to witness one of the biggest moments of Vincent and Erin's lives happening in real time. With the groomsmen and bridal party standing up front, you could feel how deeply supported they were by everyone there. The ceremony was full of words from family and friends, and of course from Vincent and Erin themselves. Their vows were funny, light-hearted, heartfelt, and incredibly real, some of the best exchanges I have ever seen at a wedding. With the Palm Springs mountains behind them as the backdrop, the whole setting felt surreal. And once they said "I do," they walked out with a perfectly executed twirl-and-dip kiss before heading to the golf course for photos that looked absolutely magical.

Dinner & Dancing

Dinner and dancing were incredible from start to finish and honestly felt like the emotional peak of the entire weekend. The grand entrance was electric, and from there the night unfolded with one unforgettable moment after another. I gave my best man speech, mostly in a blur, but with so much love, pride, and gratitude for Vincent and Erin. We watched emotional family dances, laughed through hilarious toasts, and celebrated with people who had all played a role in their story. Dan Hatch's speech was especially memorable, long, funny, thoughtful, and perfectly him. One detail I loved was seeing Parth and Quinn at the family table. They were genuinely shocked to be seated there, but it felt completely right and made the night even more special for all of us. There were also little in-between moments that mattered just as much: catching up with close friends, seeing my mom and family smiling nonstop, and taking it all in on the dance floor. By the end of the night, all I felt was gratitude for the people in that room and for being part of such a beautiful beginning for them.

This weekend was more than a wedding. It was a full-circle family reunion, a celebration of love, and one of the happiest chapters of my life. From quiet mornings getting ready, to the rush right before the ceremony, to the loudest and happiest moments on the dance floor, every part of this trip carried real meaning. We laughed, celebrated, reconnected, and made memories across multiple generations of family and friends. Looking back, what stands out most is not just how beautiful everything looked, but how deeply everyone cared for Vincent and Erin and showed up for them in every way. It was one of those rare life moments where everything aligns: people, place, timing, and love. I will always be grateful that I got to experience it up close.
