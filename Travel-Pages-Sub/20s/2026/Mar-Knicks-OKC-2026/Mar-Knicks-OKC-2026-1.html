Knicks vs OKC

  • With Quinn
  • March 4th, 2026
  • Madison Square Garden

Game three out of three for my 2026 Knicks season. I went with Quinn to Madison Square Garden to watch the Knicks take on OKC, one of the best teams in the league. We spotted a ton of celebrities, ate some good old Mike's Hot Honey chicken, and got to watch two great teams play at the Garden. I am not the biggest basketball fan, but three Knicks games in one season might say otherwise. What a great time.
