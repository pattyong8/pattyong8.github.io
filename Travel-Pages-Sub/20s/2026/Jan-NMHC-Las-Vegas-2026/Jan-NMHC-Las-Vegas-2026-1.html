  • With Olive Tree Holdings Team
  • January 24th-27th, 2026

This was my first NMHC, and boy did it not disappoint. There was food, networking, vibes, and of course, Vegas. Made a whole lot of good connections and great memories.

I actually flew into Vegas on Saturday, a day earlier than originally planned, because there was supposed to be some pretty bad weather on Sunday. Turns out that was absolutely the right call. A lot of people ended up getting delayed, canceled, or grounded trying to fly in on Sunday, while I was already comfortably in Vegas.

Getting there early also gave me the chance to try Raku, a restaurant off the Strip that my mom and her friends had been telling me about. It was a Japanese yakitori style restaurant with a bunch of different small dishes. Everything was very good. It did not necessarily blow my mind, but I was glad I finally got to try it.

The next day, I had some time to enjoy Vegas before NMHC really got going. I went to Hell's Kitchen for dinner, which felt like a very appropriate Vegas experience, and then headed to Caesars Palace to see Absinthe.

Absinthe was one of the hottest shows in Vegas at the time and was basically a more intimate Cirque du Soleil type experience inside a tent. The performers were incredibly close to the audience, and the entire show was funny, ridiculous, and impressive. Definitely one of the more memorable Vegas shows I have seen.

Once NMHC really got going, the networking and events kicked into full gear. One of the coolest was CBRE's event at Allegiant Stadium, where they rented out the entire stadium and let everyone go down onto the field. I did not attempt a field goal, but I did try one of the football passing drills. Turns out I am still terrible at football, and basically nothing went in, but at least I tried.

More importantly, the conference was a great opportunity to meet a ton of people and catch up with friends like Liam, Morgan, and plenty of others in the industry. For my first NMHC, it was a pretty great introduction to what the week is all about.
