Danny & Sab's Wedding

  • With Seattle Crew
  • July 31st to August 2nd, 2026
  • Seattle, Washington

Danny and Sabrina's wedding was one of the highlights of 2026. I've known Danny since college, and I've known many of the people at the wedding since we all started interning at Amazon in the summer of 2018. Along the way, that group has only grown as we've met more friends and brought more people into the crew. What a great way to bring everyone back together and celebrate the marriage of two lovely people.

San Francisco Layover

My trip actually started with a layover in San Francisco before heading to Seattle. I spent some time with Ian and Nick talking about the future of Scott Street Capital, and after our first in person meeting together, we decided to grab dinner. Somehow, we were able to walk into Nari, a one Michelin star restaurant and one of my favorite Thai restaurants in the country. I really did not expect us to get in, which made it even more special. Nari always does such a fantastic job. The flavors are incredibly bold, everything is perfectly cooked, and it was a great meal to cap off our time together.

I also unexpectedly got to see Stephanie while I was in town. She saw me on Find My Friends and insisted that we hang out. I really wasn't planning to see anyone outside of Ian and Nick during such a quick stop, but I was so glad she reached out. It ended up being really nice to catch up and spend some time together before I continued on to Seattle.

Getting to Seattle

Picture #5
Picture #6

Getting to Seattle ended up being a little more complicated than expected. I was supposed to take an early flight from San Francisco the morning of the wedding, but my flight was canceled. Thankfully, I was able to talk to a representative who moved me onto another airline that morning and somehow still got me into Seattle around my original 8 or 9 a.m. arrival time. That meant I could get to Noah's, sleep for a little bit, and not have to rush straight to the wedding.

Once I woke up, Noah and I went over to Alex's house to grab his overalls, where I got to see Alex, Danny, and Logan. It was so nice to see everyone again and start the weekend surrounded by people who have become such a big part of my Seattle crew.

Before getting ready, Noah and I picked up sandwiches for Noah, Stephanie, and me from a great Italian sandwich shop that reminded me a lot of All'Antico Vinaio in Italy. After eating, we got dressed and headed to a nearby hotel where the wedding shuttle was picking everyone up. That was really when the reunion started. I began running into friends I hadn't seen in a very long time, and it felt so good reconnecting with people who had been a big part of earlier chapters of my life. I even ran into an old friend from Berkeley who didn't recognize me at first on the bus, but after the ceremony we reconnected and he finally remembered me.

The wedding venue itself was absolutely beautiful. It was about 45 minutes outside Seattle in the mountains, centered around this beautiful home with an incredible entrance and backyard. The ceremony was set right alongside a creek, with natural running water directly behind the altar. It was honestly one of the most beautiful wedding venues I had ever been to.

Seattle also gave Danny and Sabrina the perfect day. The sky was completely blue, the weather was fantastic, and the entire setting felt idyllic. Between the mountains, trees, creek, and beautiful weather, it was hard to imagine a better backdrop for their wedding.

The ceremony and cocktail hour were also just an opportunity to spend quality time with so many people I hadn't seen in a while. There were old friends, newer Seattle friends, and people from different parts of my life all in one place. We got some great group photos, including photo 13 with Danny and Sabrina and the crew, and of course the boys had to get some photos of our own in pictures 16 through 18.

Dinner and the reception were an absolute blast. The food was genuinely fantastic, and photo 19 captures the group I spent much of dinner with. I sat across from Logan, got to spend more time with Tim and others, and we all got to celebrate Danny and Sabrina together.

Once the music started, everyone really got into it. There were so many great sing along songs, and before long the entire group was dancing. They also had these light up foam sticks that everyone became completely obsessed with, which somehow made the dance floor even more fun. Even the cake was incredible. Between the food, music, dancing, and all the people there, it was just a really, really good time.

Somehow, the ride home was almost as fun as the wedding itself. Noah basically took over the bus and became our DJ, jumping between house music, techno, and ridiculous sing along songs. Everyone was completely bought in, and the energy on that bus ride back was electric.

The night still wasn't over. We ended up at a bar near Noah and Stephanie's house for the after party, which was especially convenient because we could eventually just walk home. We grabbed a few more drinks, spent more time with friends and family, and got to have some of the deeper conversations that tend to happen toward the end of a long night. Photo 30 was basically the finale: we closed down the bar with the family. An incredible way to end the wedding night.

The next morning, Tim and Sam hosted a post wedding breakfast with an amazing spread of quiches, cupcakes, and a make your own lox bagel setup. Somehow, none of us were really that hungover, so it ended up being a genuinely enjoyable morning rather than just everyone recovering from the night before. It was a great, relaxed way to get everyone back together one more time after the wedding.

The rest of the day turned into a full Seattle activity day, even though I barely took any photos. Noah and I went climbing with Alex and AJ at Seattle Bouldering Project, which has a special place in my heart because it is really where my climbing journey started. In between activities, we stopped at this great little taco spot with incredibly cheap tacos and wonderful food.

Later, we went golfing with Logan and Danny at a short par three course. I definitely did not play particularly well, while it turned out Logan was incredible. More importantly, this was one of the first trips I remember where Noah was really starting to get into golf. It became the beginning of another activity that we could do together and something we would continue to share after the trip.

I flew home the next morning, but the entire weekend was such a great reminder of the friends I have in Seattle and around the country.
