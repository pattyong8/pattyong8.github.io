Irvine Summer

  • With Family
  • July and August 2026
  • Irvine, California

As I usually do, I came home for the summer to enjoy some time with family and friends. This summer was particularly significant because there was a new member of the family. Annabelle was born in the summer of 2026, and I came back a few times to see her, spend time with her, and enjoy my new role as the funcle. What a wonderful milestone for Vinny and Erin and for our entire family.

Annabelle

Picture #2

The summer of 2026 was really defined by getting to spend time with Annabelle, my new niece. She was the cutest, most precious baby I had ever met. I went over to Vinny and Erin's many times just to see her, hold her, and spend time with the newest member of our family.

I really had not spent much time around babies before, so I learned a lot during these visits. Even something as simple as learning how to comfortably hold and take care of a baby was new to me. It was really special getting to know Annabelle during the first few months of her life and watching Vinny and Erin settle into being parents.

Of course, being home also meant eating incredibly well. While I was visiting and meeting Annabelle, Vinny decided to cook up a storm. He had bought a bunch of steaks from Costco a few weeks earlier and saved the rib caps that he had butchered himself. They were incredible.

Mom and Doug also made fresh pesto pasta, and Doug brought some of his signature homemade bread. We even finished everything off with snickerdoodles. A pretty classic family meal where there was absolutely no shortage of food.

Vinny, Mom, and I also went to Chubby Cattle, an all you can eat Japanese barbecue spot that Vinny was really excited to try. They had an amazing selection of meats and other dishes. It was definitely expensive, but everything was very tasty.

I also tried uni mentaiko for the first time. It was good, although I think I still prefer having uni fresh rather than in mentaiko form.

Mom and I went to try Tim Ho Wan together. The dim sum was good, and I was particularly impressed by the har gow. They were really tightly put together and well made.

A lot of the summer was simply home cooked meals with the family. Mom made jambalaya during another visit to Vinny and Erin's, and Doug made fresh homemade pizzas.

Picture 18 was easily my favorite: sausage, mushrooms, and cheese. Just can't get enough of that combination.

As you can probably tell from the photos, there was a lot of food this summer. Mom is always cooking something wonderful whenever I'm home.

It was also incredibly hot, so Mom made some aiyu, which was the perfect refreshing drink for the weather.

Other activities

But there was also plenty of time with family in between all the meals. I got to spend more time with Annabelle, Davis and I hung out by the pool, and we went to visit Mom while she was pet sitting at a house.

We also went to Diamond Jamboree for bingsu, which is always a good summer dessert.

Vinny and I went out to play nine holes at the par three course near John Wayne Airport. We ended up finishing within one stroke of each other. I lost it a little toward the end, but overall I was playing and hitting the ball pretty well, which was exciting.

Before I left, we went to see The Odyssey together in 70mm IMAX. It was one of the best moviegoing experiences I'd had in a while. Christopher Nolan shot the movie in IMAX, and you could really feel the scale of it on that screen.

Our seats on the left side of the theater were definitely not ideal, but it almost didn't matter. Some of the images were so immersive that it felt like you were actually inside the scene. It had been a long time since I'd had an IMAX experience that felt like that.

Between welcoming Annabelle into the family, spending time with everyone, eating way too much good food, golfing with Vinny, and simply being back home, this was a particularly special Irvine summer.
