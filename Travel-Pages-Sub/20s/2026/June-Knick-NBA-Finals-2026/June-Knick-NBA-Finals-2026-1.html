Knicks NBA Finals

  • With New York Friends
  • June 3rd-14th, 2026
  • New York City

I am definitely not the biggest sports fan, but when your local team makes a deep run in the playoffs, it is hard not to get caught up in the excitement. Coincidentally, I had actually gone to three Knicks games that season and had the chance to see them play against some of the best teams in the league, including OKC. So this ended up being a particularly fun year to follow the Knicks, and I was glad I got to be a part of the excitement in New York.

A lot of our New York friends got together throughout the playoffs to watch the games. There were plenty of nights at outdoor bars, watching TVs from the street and gathering with increasingly large groups of Knicks fans. Every restaurant and bar seemed to know how big of a deal the games were becoming. TVs were moved around, outdoor setups were reconfigured, and seemingly every available screen was positioned so people could watch from every possible angle.

One of my favorite memories was a game that Parth and I had started watching with friends. The Knicks were down by around 25 points at halftime, and our group was pretty demoralized. We eventually decided to call it and head home early.

Of course, while we were heading home, the Knicks started making an incredible comeback. By the time Parth and I realized what was happening, we had just gotten home and there were only about five minutes left in the game. We immediately turned around and rushed back out of the house to the nearest bar so we could watch the ending surrounded by other fans. Somehow, we made it just in time to see the Knicks finish the comeback. The energy was incredible, especially after we had basically given up on the game less than an hour earlier.

A Good Spot

Picture #7
Picture #8

As the run continued, the crowds around the city just kept getting bigger. For one of the games, we went to an outdoor viewing area and arrived really early so we could stake out a good spot. Eventually, so many people showed up that the streets became completely packed. The crowd started spilling into traffic, and the viewing event ultimately had to be shut down. It was hilarious, although also incredibly disappointing considering how early we had gotten there specifically to make sure we had a spot.

For the final games, the streets were packed in a way I had rarely seen before. It reminded me of being in San Francisco when the Warriors were making their championship runs. There is something special about watching an entire city get behind its team, even if you are not normally the biggest sports fan. It was an exciting moment to experience and a great way to feel like a New Yorker.
