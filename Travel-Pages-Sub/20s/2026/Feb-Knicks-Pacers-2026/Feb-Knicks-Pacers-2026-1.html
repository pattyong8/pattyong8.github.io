Knicks vs Pacers

  • With Elijah
  • Feb 2026
  • Madison Square Garden

Game two out of three for my 2026 Knicks season. I randomly got a last second ticket after someone couldn't make the Knicks versus Pacers game, so I brought Elijah, one of my good real estate friends who I've gotten to know and hang out with a lot in New York.

This ended up being an incredible game to attend. It was extremely close and went all the way to double overtime, with Elijah and me going off the entire time. Sadly, the Knicks didn't pull out the win, but the energy at Madison Square Garden was electric. Certainly not game four of the NBA Finals, but still one of the most exciting basketball games I've gotten to see in person.
