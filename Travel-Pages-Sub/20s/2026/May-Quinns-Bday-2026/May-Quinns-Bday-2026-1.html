Quinn's Birthday

  • With New York Friends
  • May 30th, 2026
  • US Open Tennis Courts and Chinatown

Quinn's birthday present may have been half a year late, but it was one of the coolest yet. We rented three tennis courts at the US Open and brought together a ton of friends to celebrate Quinn's birthday during some hopefully better weather. Turns out the day was incredibly windy, but we still got everyone together, played a ton of tennis, and finished the day eating our way through Chinatown. Happy birthday, Quinn!

We had people from all different parts of Quinn's life come out for tennis. Gabby and her friends joined, along with some of Quinn and Parth's tennis friends from college, Quinn's sister, our other New York friends, and even Rishi's sister. We had five really good tennis players and plenty of intermediate players and beginners, so it was really just about getting everyone on the courts and having a good time.

I also came prepared in my full tracksuit. Parth had given me a Pomona Pitzer Nike top, and it turned out I had matching bottoms from City on water polo, so naturally I had to wear the full set.

We spent a lot of the day playing Around the World, while some of the better players got together for Dingles. Mostly, though, everyone just hit around, rotated between the three courts, and enjoyed getting to play tennis at the US Open with such a big group of friends. Despite the wind, it ended up being a great way to celebrate Quinn.

After tennis, we headed to Chinatown and basically turned the rest of the afternoon into a food tour. We bounced around trying duck buns, spicy dumplings, sheng jian bao, steamed rice rolls, and a ton of other great food.

It was a pretty perfect way to end the day. Lots of tennis, way too much food, and a big group of people we care about all getting together to celebrate Quinn's birthday.
