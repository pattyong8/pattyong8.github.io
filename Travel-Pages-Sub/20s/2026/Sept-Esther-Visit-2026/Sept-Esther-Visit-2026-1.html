Esther Visit NYC

  • With Esther
  • September 6th, 2026
  • New York City

Esther came into town over Labor Day weekend in September, and it was the perfect opportunity to catch up. We ate, we laughed, we drank, and even got to watch her favorite tennis player, Ben Shelton, play at the U.S. Open.

When Esther told me she was coming into town, I didn’t really have enough time to make a reservation anywhere. But since it was Labor Day weekend, we figured we could try our luck walking in somewhere. Red Hook Tavern had been on my list for a while, and Esther has more recently become a burger person, so it felt like the perfect opportunity to finally give it a try.

We showed up about an hour before opening and the line was already almost 15 people long. We decided to stick it out, figuring we still had a pretty good chance of getting in. By 30 minutes before opening, the line had grown to nearly 75 people, which made us feel a lot better about how early we had arrived. It paid off. We made the first round of seating and somehow got the last two person table indoors.

We both ordered the Red Hook Tavern burger, which is what they are known for, and it was honestly a very good burger. The dry aged beef patty was delicious, and the cheese was a great balance to the fattiness and flavor of the meat. The bun was probably the weakest part. It was basically a regular white bun, and we were hoping for something more interesting, maybe a brioche or pretzel bun that could better complement the flavor of the burger. The fries were also just okay and surprisingly a little cold considering the restaurant had just opened. Still, it was a great experience and fun to finally try what a lot of people call one of New York’s best burgers.

After lunch, we decided to walk through Red Hook toward one of the barbecue spots that people say is among the best in the city. Along the way, we stopped at a bar with live music playing. The performer was this hilarious older man whose songs were filled with sexual innuendos. We shared an Aperol Spritz, listened to him play, and eventually continued on toward the barbecue spot.

U.S. Open + Barbecue

When we finally got to the barbecue spot, the line was way too long. But it turned out we had ended up in exactly the right place because right next door was an outdoor viewing area showing the U.S. Open. We grabbed some frozen honeydew drinks, sat outside on a beautiful day, and watched Ben Shelton play, which was especially perfect because he is Esther’s favorite tennis player.

Parth eventually joined us, and we spent the afternoon drinking frozen honeydews and watching the match together. Those drinks were dangerously good, and we definitely got quite drunk off of them, but they were perfect in the moment. We eventually ended the night with some good old barbecue, a fitting end to a full day eating and drinking our way through Red Hook.
