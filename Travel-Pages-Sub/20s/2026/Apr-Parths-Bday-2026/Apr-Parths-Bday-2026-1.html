Parth's Birthday

  • With New York Friends
  • April 8th, 2026
  • Pizza Crawl and Dave & Buster's

For Parth's birthday in 2026, we decided to focus on two of his favorite things: pizza and arcades. We brought together friends from different parts of his life for what was supposed to be a pizza crawl followed by Dave & Buster's. We ate some good food, played a ton of games, and got to spend some quality time together celebrating Parth.

The Pizza Crawl

We unfortunately don't have many pictures from the pizza crawl, although calling it a crawl might be generous. We started the night at a bar where everyone gathered and waited for Parth to arrive. In classic Parth fashion, he was late, so basically everyone was already there by the time he showed up. John did a great job of eventually getting the birthday boy there.

After a few drinks, we started the pizza crawl at Artichoke Pizza. Turns out Artichoke may have been the worst possible place to start a pizza crawl because the slices were so heavy and rich that everyone was basically done eating after our first stop. So instead of forcing ourselves through more pizza, we abandoned the crawl and headed straight to Dave & Buster's.

I also got Parth a birthday sash to wear throughout the night, which he sported through both pizza and the arcade. Before heading inside Dave & Buster's, we stopped for another round of drinks, where the bartender made Parth a ridiculous birthday shot. He clearly did not enjoy it at all, which made it even funnier, but he powered through and drank it anyway. We also managed to get a great group photo before heading inside.

It was actually Parth's first time at Dave & Buster's. He had always talked about how much he liked arcades, so it felt like the perfect place to take him for his birthday.

We played basketball, Hungry Hungry Hippos, racing games, and pretty much anything else that caught our attention. I somehow kept winning the Hot Wheels game, which became increasingly funny as the night went on. Mostly, though, we just bounced between games, competed against each other, and had a really good time hanging out.

After Dave & Buster's, a smaller group of us headed home to keep celebrating. Emily had made Parth a really nice strawberry birthday cake, so Parth, Emily, Quinn, Gabby, and I finished the night together with cake at home.

What a great way to bring everyone together and celebrate one of our favorite people. Happy birthday, Parth!
