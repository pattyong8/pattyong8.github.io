ONGtheroad

A digital scrapbook

This website is dedicated to preserving all my spontaneous adventures, precious memories, and life changing experiences. Although the main goal is to create a repository of my life, this site also serves as a self-imposed accountability measure, forcing me to step beyond my comfort zone and explore what life has to offer. Please explore the site and take a trip down memory road with me!

About me

My name is Patrick Ong, and my goal with this website is to capture all the amazing moments in my life in one central location. A few of my passions are rock climbing (currently bouldering and lead), scuba diving, skiing, teaching and mentoring, and traveling. I was introduced to climbing in Seattle over the summer of 2018, and since then it has been a large part of my life. One of my life goals is to become a dive master, which is hard but attainable.

I am an extrovert by nature and love exploring new things, especially with friends and family.

Where I am headed

Another goal of mine is to visit at least half of the countries in the world. It was inspired by my Japan, Taiwan, and Thailand post college trip and reinforced by my trip to Croatia in 2022. I had such an amazing time backpacking through Southeast Asia and cannot wait to see the rest of the world.

I want to do something in life that I will be proud of. Whether I am impacting the lives of those I mentor or contributing to society in a meaningful way, I hope I can look back and know that I lived it to the fullest.
